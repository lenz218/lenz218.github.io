What the tool does

It uploads photo posts created by the owner as drafts to the owner's own TikTok inbox via the official TikTok Content Posting API (upload mode). It never publishes automatically. Every post is reviewed, edited and published manually by the account owner in the TikTok app.

Who may use it

The tool is not offered to other people. No third-party accounts are connected.

Data
